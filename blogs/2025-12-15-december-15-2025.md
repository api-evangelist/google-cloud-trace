Feature The Trace Explorer has been updated to include annotations that let you identify App Hub-registered services and workloads. The link provided with a service or workload lets you open the corresponding Application Monitoring dashboard. To learn more, see the following documents: Find and explore traces describes how to use the Trace Explorer page to filter and explore your trace data.
